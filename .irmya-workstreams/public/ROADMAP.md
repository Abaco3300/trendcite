## TCP-P005 — Feedback & Adoption Learning
- bug/feature/use-case/Pro-interest separation
- voluntary attribution

## Outcome acceptance
TrendCite Public remains useful independently of Pro, free/MIT/local-first, easier to discover/install/use, reliable for humans and agents, evidence-first, privacy-preserving, tested and maintainable.
