## Product objective

Turn TrendCite from a validated evidence-first local/OSS intelligence engine into a stable hosted TrendCite Pro v1 service that can continuously monitor configured sources, persist signals and tenant relevance, run scheduled Radars, and ultimately reach production readiness.

## Authorized current outcome

Bring TrendCite Pro v1 from the validated runtime state to a stable persistent non-production hosted environment, then continue through the remaining pre-production capabilities required for production readiness, stopping only at genuine Human Gates.

## Product principles

- Evidence before synthesis.
- Deterministic and versioned scoring/identity where possible.
- Honest uncertainty and explicit INSUFFICIENT_DATA.
- Tenant isolation.
- Durable idempotent execution.
- No unauthorized publishing.
- No scraping workaround for restricted platforms.
- Shared service does not imply shared project state.

## Current hard boundaries

Not authorized without a genuine Human Gate:
- production activation with external impact;
- live billing or charge;
- legal/contractual commitment;
- real third-party communication/commitment;
- unapproved spend or material budget increase;
- new credential/protected-data authority not already granted;
- destructive or hard-to-reverse action.
